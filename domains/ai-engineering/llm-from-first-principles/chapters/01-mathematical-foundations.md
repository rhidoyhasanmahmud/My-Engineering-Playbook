## Overview

This chapter establishes the mathematical vocabulary used to reason about neural networks and language models.

## Learning Objectives

- Understand the motivation and core terminology.
- Explain the important algorithms and trade-offs.
- Implement or evaluate a small, verifiable example.
- Document limitations and engineering implications.

## Topics Covered

- Scalars, vectors, matrices and tensors
- NumPy arrays, shapes, broadcasting and dot products
- Matrix multiplication and cosine similarity
- Derivatives, gradients and optimization intuition
