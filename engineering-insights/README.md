# Engineering Insights

Short, original notes from engineering blogs, technical articles, and company engineering write-ups.

## Purpose

Capture what I understood, why it matters, and where I could apply it. Keep notes concise, attribute the original source, and distinguish the author's claims from my own interpretations.

## Note format

- **Title:**
- **Source:** Company / author, article URL, publication date (if available)
- **Domain / Tags:**
- **Problem:** What challenge was addressed?
- **Approach:** What did the authors do?
- **Key takeaways:** 3–5 concise points
- **My understanding:** What I learned in my own words
- **Practical application:** Where could I use it?
- **Trade-offs / open questions:** What remains unclear or context-dependent?

## Guidelines

- Write in your own words; do not reproduce entire articles or proprietary diagrams.
- Link the original source and date the note.
- Cross-link durable concepts to the relevant domain or System Design notes.
- Organize by year or topic only when enough notes accumulate.
